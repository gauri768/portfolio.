7 Days Fusion 360 Learning Plan

Day 1 – Introduction & Interface

What is Fusion 360 (CAD, CAM, CAE basics)
Workspace overview (Design, Solid, Surface)
Navigation (zoom, pan, orbit)
Create your first sketch
👉 Practice:
Draw basic shapes (rectangle, circle, line)

Day 2 – Sketching (2D Basics)

Sketch tools: Line, Circle, Arc, Rectangle
Constraints (very important): Coincident, Parallel, Perpendicular
Dimensions
👉 Practice:
Draw a fully constrained sketch (no blue lines)

Day 3 – Advanced Sketch Practice

Offset, Trim, Extend
Mirror & Pattern
Complex sketches
👉 Practice:
Draw: Gear outline (simple), Bracket shape

Day 4 – 3D Modeling (Core Day 🚀)

Convert sketch → 3D using: Extrude, Revolve
Modify tools: Fillet, Chamfer
👉 Practice:
Make: Cylinder, Bolt-like shape

Day 5 – Assembly & Motion

Components vs Bodies
Joint tool
Motion study
👉 Practice:
Create: Simple hinge mechanism

Day 6 – Engineering Practice

Create real parts: Spur gear (basic), Plate with holes
Learn: Measure tool, Inspect
👉 Practice:
Design a laser-cut plate

Day 7 – Final Project 🎯

Combine everything
